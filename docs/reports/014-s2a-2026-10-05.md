     - reads every `brake:*` key first;
     - folds held alerts;
     - keeps `posting_tick`'s problem and outage guards (card 039's bad `STATE_READS` is one such problem);
     - works out due tasks from Dict state only.
     Its one task, `assisted`, runs `bot/posting.assisted_tick`: `posting_tick`'s per-account body, unchanged. A connected profile shows "upload_post, waiting for S2b's publishing".
   - **The brake** (`brake:<scope>` Dict keys, written first) with `/pause all` and `/go all`.
     - With Neon down, the reply says "recorded in the brake only".
     - `posting_daily` repairs a key and its row where they differ: the newer wins. Under `/pause all` it converges to paused and alerts. A missing key is never restored blindly.
   - **The autopilot service** (the one writer, with append-only history):
     - presets; an override turns the preset into `custom`;
     - any person's change to the Review dial needs a reason;
     - a Publish change rewrites the publish path (`posting:publish:<account>`);
     - a missing row reads as Hands-on.
     Routes `GET/PUT /admin/accounts/{id}/autopilot` (a person actor in the header); CLI `clipforge autopilot show|set|preset`.
   - **The policy gate v1** (pure, 21 golden cases in EN and ES) runs log-only on each assisted pick (`GATE_ENFORCE` off). `clipforge policy dry-run` and `GET /admin/policy/dry-run` list what it would hold and write nothing. Routing (`review/routing.py`, `db/review.py`) covers the windows and the deterministic spot checks.
