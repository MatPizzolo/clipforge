2. **Summary (HK-1):**
   - **The hook library** (`hooks/library.py`, the one writer of the hook tables, over `db/hooks.py`):
     - drafts, versions (an edit writes v+1), approve, retire, share to the blueprint, and per-account weights with a reason;
     - every change writes its `hook_events` row in the same transaction, with S2's actor check.
   - **`SqlHookFreezer`** implements S3c's `HookFreezer` on the caller's connection: idempotent, a no-op without a library, and an error if another experiment already holds the freeze.
   - **The seeds:** `clipforge hooks seed` writes spec §4.1's six patterns per clips account (one control, five others, weight 1.0, `system:migration`). It is idempotent and has a dry run.
   - **The rotation on jobs:** `service.create_job` freezes the account's rotation on `JobInput.hooks`. It discards one sent by the caller, and any error leaves none with `Job.hooks_note = "unavailable"`. The pick (`hooks/rotation.py`) is pure and seeded, so HK-2 can use it unchanged.
   - **Control stamps with `HOOK_VARIANTS` off; clip output is unchanged:**
     - `package` writes `PackagedClip.hook_stamp` and `versions.hook_rotation` into `metadata.json`;
     - enqueue writes the stamp columns on `content_items` through `db/posting.py`;
     - both derive the stamp through one function, `stamp_for`.
   - **Routes and the CLI:**
     - `/admin/accounts/{id}/hooks` and `/admin/hooks/…` sit next to #52's `/admin/*` routes, behind the bearer token, with #52's `person` actor;
     - `clipforge hooks list|show|add|edit|approve|retire|share|weight|seed|stats` (stats arrives with HK-2);
     - `web/openapi.json` and the client are regenerated.
