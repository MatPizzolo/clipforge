# Content sourcing policy

ClipForge is built for content we are allowed to use:

- Our own channels
- Creators who hired us, or paid clipping programs that allow it
- Creative Commons licensed videos (credit the creator in `post.md`)
- Public domain material

Every job records its source and permission basis in `metadata.json`
(`permission: own | creator_agreement | clipping_program | cc_by | public_domain`).

Out of scope: features designed to evade copyright detection (mirroring, pitch or speed shifts, crops or overlays meant to beat Content ID), and bulk reposting of other people's content without permission. Beyond the legal risk, platforms strike, demonetize or ban accounts for it.
