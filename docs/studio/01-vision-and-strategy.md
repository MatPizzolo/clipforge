# 01: Vision and strategy

The numbers below were checked on 2026-09-29 and re-checked against primary sources in the kickoff review the same day. Platform programs change often, so the dashboard must hold them as **editable settings, not constants**.

## Goal

A studio that runs 20+ short-video accounts from one dashboard:
- **Produce** videos: podcast clips, AI stories, band stories and AI-presenter videos.
- **Review** them at the right level for each account.
- **Publish** to TikTok, Reels, Shorts and Facebook Reels.
- **Measure** views, followers, clicks, sales and cost.
- **Scale what wins:** more of a winning format, and translations into new languages.

Success after 90 days means:
- at least 5 accounts posting daily without manual uploads;
- cost and revenue per account visible in the dashboard;
- at least one account in a platform payout program.

**Rule: post every video to every platform an account has enabled.** Extra platforms cost almost nothing. The "main" platform only decides which rules shape the format:
- stories run 61–90 s so they qualify for TikTok Rewards;
- everything stays original so it passes YouTube's inauthentic-content rule.

## Account types

### 1. Podcast clips (realtalk.clipsdaily, more later)
- **Producer:** the existing ClipForge pipeline.
- **Platforms:** TikTok (growth), Instagram (brand deals and affiliate), Shorts, Facebook.
- **Money:** paid clipping (from the creator, or Whop campaigns), then Skool and infoproduct affiliate links in the men's-growth niche, then brand deals. Platform payouts are a bonus: reused-content rules make clips a weak fit for YPP and TikTok Rewards.
- **Format:** 30–60 s, with the creator credited in every caption.

### 2. AI narrated stories (one or more niches)
- **Producer:** script → voice → images or b-roll → captions → render.
- **Platforms:** **TikTok and Facebook make the money** (61–90 s, "Part 1/2" series). Shorts adds reach and long-lasting views, with long-form compilations once in YPP. Instagram gets cross-posts.
- **Script sources:** Claude originals and research-based facts. **Adapted Reddit posts are out.** Reddit requires prior approval and a contract for commercial use, and the posts belong to their authors.
- **Candidate niches:**
  1. unsolved mysteries / strange history
  2. money cautionary tales
  3. stoic / men's-growth parables (shared audience with realtalk)
  4. forgotten sports stories
  5. weird science / "what if"
- **Launch one, measure, then add more.** The concrete portfolio and launch waves are in [07-channel-portfolio.md](07-channel-portfolio.md).

### 3. Music bands ("a band you've never heard of from <country>")
- **Producer:** research → script → voice → licensed photos or generated **art** → render, as a **music-free master**. The song is added in the app from the platform's licensed library, and the item carries a "suggested sound" field.
- **Platforms:** Instagram first (collab posts with the band), TikTok for discovery, Shorts second.
- **Money:** paid features (disclosed), music-gear affiliate links, and later a curator brand.
- **Rules:**
  - Never generate photoreal images of the real band members.
  - Photos come from Wikimedia Commons (CC-BY or CC-BY-SA, with attribution) or with the band's written permission.
  - Official videos are used only with the band's permission.

### 4. AI-avatar affiliate
- **Producer:** a synthetic persona (face plus designed voice) presents products, infoproducts or Skool communities.
- **Platforms:** TikTok Shop first, then Reels (link in bio or DM links), Shorts (Shopping), Facebook.
- **Money:** commission per sale, tracked by per-video links.
- **Compliance, never optional:**
  - The AI host is openly AI: platform AI labels on, and "AI host" in the bio.
  - **No first-person testimonials** such as "I used it and…". The FTC's 2024 rule bans fake testimonials.
  - No health claims for supplements, and **no earnings claims** for money-making products.
  - Every sponsored or affiliate video carries #ad.
- **Each account is a distinct niche brand.** No networks of near-duplicate pages built to survive bans; platforms treat that as coordinated inauthentic behavior.

### 5. AI model / influencer (lifestyle)
- A disclosed synthetic persona (travel, fashion, fitness lifestyle) posting carousels and reels.
- **Money:** brand deals, affiliate storefronts, disclosed sponsored posts.
- **Rules:** provenance metadata kept (never stripped to pass as human), synthetic-only training data, no swaps onto real people's footage. Concepts are in 07.

### Later: own products
The channels feed a funnel (bio page → email → own course or community). It's planned in 08 §5 and built in S14.

## Content rules for all accounts

1. **Originality over volume.** YouTube's "inauthentic content" rule (renamed from "repetitious" on 2025-07-15; clarified in July 2026 into template content, unsatisfying content, and AI personas advising on sensitive topics) judges mass-produced content across the whole channel. Generators must vary structure, pacing, voice and visuals, never repeating a fixed template.
2. **Disclosure.** Synthetic voices, avatars and generated realistic footage set each platform's AI flag. Real podcast clips don't need it.
3. **One video, one account.** The same video never goes to two accounts in the same language. Translations for a different-language account are allowed.
4. **Licenses tracked per asset.** Every image, clip, voice, music bed and model used carries its license and attribution.
5. **No copyright-evasion features** (CLAUDE.md rule 9).

## Review policy (tiered)

| Tier | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| `review` | New account, new format or new producer version; every affiliate or sponsored video for the first 30 days | Telegram asks for approval per video |
| `sample` | After 30 days and 50+ approved videos with a reject rate under 10% | Auto-post; Telegram shows ~1 in 10 for a spot check, plus a daily digest |
| `auto` | Owner sets it explicitly | Auto-post, digest only |

Since 2026-10-01 the tier is the **Review dial** of each account's autopilot (ADR-48): the system suggests moving up the ladder and the owner taps; demotions are automatic. A new producer version sends only the **first 5 items** per account to `review` (ADR-49); a format change, the first 10.

The **automated policy gate runs in every tier**. It checks disclosure flags, #ad on affiliate videos, credits, license manifests, duplicates across accounts, and banned claims (health or earnings). A failure always drops the video back to `review`.

## Languages and translation

- English (US) and Spanish accounts are separate accounts, each with its own voice and handle.
- **Translate winners:** a video in the top 10% of its account by views after 7 days gets queued for dubbing into the paired account in the other language. That means a new voice, re-timed captions and, for avatars, a new lip-sync.
